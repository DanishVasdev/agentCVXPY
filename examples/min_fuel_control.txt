A spacecraft moves along a line. Its state is described by position and velocity, and it is
controlled by a sequence of 10 discrete-time thrust commands, one per time step, indexed
0 through 9. Each thrust command can be positive or negative.

The spacecraft starts at time 0 at position 0 with velocity 0. At each time step t, the
position at t+1 equals the position at t plus the velocity at t. The velocity at t+1 equals
the velocity at t plus the thrust command at t.

Fuel consumed by a thrust command is proportional to its absolute value, with proportionality
constant 1. Each individual thrust command's magnitude cannot exceed 1.

Find the sequence of thrust commands that minimizes total fuel consumed (the sum of the
absolute values of all 10 thrust commands) such that at time step 10 the spacecraft reaches
exactly position 6 with exactly zero velocity.
