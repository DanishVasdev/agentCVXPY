An investor has $1 to allocate across three assets. The expected returns are 0.10, 0.15, and 0.08
for assets 1, 2, and 3 respectively. The covariance matrix of the asset returns is
[[0.05, 0.01, 0.00], [0.01, 0.08, 0.02], [0.00, 0.02, 0.03]].
The investor wants to choose portfolio weights for the three assets that minimize portfolio risk,
measured as the variance w^T Sigma w, subject to achieving an expected portfolio return of at least 0.10,
investing the full dollar (weights summing to 1), and not allowing any short positions
(each weight must be nonnegative).
