Each chair sells for a profit of $15 and each table for a profit of $40.
Each chair requires 3 hours of carpentry and 1 hour of finishing.
Each table requires 5 hours of carpentry and 4 hours of finishing.
The factory has 120 hours of carpentry time and 60 hours of finishing time available per week.
The factory cannot produce a negative number of chairs or tables.
How many chairs and tables should be produced each week to maximize profit?
